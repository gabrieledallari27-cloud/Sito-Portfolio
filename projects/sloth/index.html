Sloth Guardian

For this solo university project focused on creature design, I created a forest guardian — a wooden and earthen sloth carrying a large willow on its back. The concept merges the sloth’s lifelong bond to its tree with the idea of a protective spirit of nature.

Modeling, texturing and rendering in ZBrush. Final compositing in Photoshop.
